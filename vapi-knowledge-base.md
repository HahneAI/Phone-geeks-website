## About Phone Geeks

Phone Geeks is a family-run phone, computer, tablet, and game console
repair shop serving the St. Louis, Missouri area, in business since 2016.

Every repair comes with a 1-year warranty covering parts and labor. The
warranty does not cover liquid damage or damage caused after the repair
by normal drops or wear. There is no prepayment required — customers pay
only once a repair is complete. Pricing is always the cost of the
replacement part plus labor, and the total is given before any work
starts.

## What We Repair

**Smartphones**: screen repair, battery replacement, charging port
repair, camera replacement, speaker/mic repair, and water damage
diagnostics (24–72 hours depending on severity).

**Computers (Mac and Windows)**: screen replacement, battery replacement,
keyboard/trackpad repair, virus removal and software fixes, data
recovery, and liquid damage cleaning.

**Tablets and iPads**: screen repair, battery replacement, and charging
port repair.

**Game consoles**, including older systems like the PSP, Nintendo DS, and
Wii U, not just current-generation consoles: HDMI port repair, disc drive
repair, overheating/fan cleaning, and controller repair.

**Also offered**: gadget buyback and recycling (trade in an old device
for cash, or drop it off for free e-waste recycling), refurbished phone
and accessory retail sales (backed by a 60-day return window), and a
free walk-in "Consult a Geek" diagnosis with no appointment needed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does water damage repair take?**
It depends on how bad the damage is, but most water damage repairs take
24–72 hours, since the device needs to be fully dried and tested before
it's put back together.

**Do I have to pay upfront?**
No. There's no prepayment — customers pay once the repair is finished and
they've had a chance to check it over.

**How is the price determined?**
Price is the cost of the replacement part plus labor, and the customer
always gets the total before any work starts.

**Do you repair iPads and tablets?**
Yes — screen, battery, and charging port repairs for iPads and most
Android tablets.

**Do you work on desktop and laptop computers?**
Yes, both Mac and Windows. Hardware issues like screens, batteries, and
keyboards, as well as software issues like viruses, slow performance, and
data recovery.

**Do you repair older game consoles?**
Yes — older systems like the PSP, Nintendo DS, and Wii U are repaired,
not just current-generation consoles.

## Tone & Style Notes for Answering

Answer like a real person at the counter, not a script. Keep it short —
one or two sentences per answer unless the caller asks for more detail.
Never quote a price or turnaround time for a specific repair from memory
— use the `get_repair_estimate` tool for that so the number always
matches the website. This document covers policy and general-knowledge
questions; the tools cover anything caller-specific (stock, pricing for a
stated device + issue, booking).
